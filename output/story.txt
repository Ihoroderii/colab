The city was bathed in a warm orange glow as dusk descended upon its concrete skyscrapers. The air was alive with the hum of activity, a cacophony of sounds that seemed to reverberate deep within Jin's chest. He stood atop the rooftop, the wind whipping his black hair into a frenzy as he gazed out over the sprawling metropolis. It was a moment of perfect solitude, one that he savored with every fiber of his being.

Jin's eyes were fixed on a figure perched on the rooftop across the way. Raven, a notorious mercenary, was a name that sent shivers down the spines of even the most hardened operatives. Her reputation was built on a foundation of ruthless efficiency and an unyielding commitment to her craft. And yet, as Jin watched her, he couldn't help but feel a spark of curiosity ignite within him. What was she doing here? And why did he feel an inexplicable sense of unease in her presence?

As the sun dipped lower in the sky, casting long shadows across the rooftop, Jin felt a presence behind him. He turned to see a figure cloaked in darkness, their features obscured by the fading light. "You shouldn't be here," the figure growled, their voice low and menacing. Jin's instincts kicked in, and he reached for the knife strapped to his belt. But before he could draw it, the figure vanished into thin air.

Raven, it seemed, had not been alone. A rival faction, one that Jin had long suspected was operating in the shadows, had just made their presence known. And as he stood there, the wind whipping his hair into a frenzy, Jin felt a sense of purpose crystallize within him. He would not be intimidated. He would not be deterred.

With a fierce determination burning within his chest, Jin turned to face Raven once more. The air seemed to crackle with tension as their eyes met, a silent understanding passing between them like a spark of electricity. And in that moment, Jin knew that he had made a decision. He would confront Raven, and he would uncover the truth about the mysterious faction that had just made its presence known. The rooftop oath had been made, and nothing would ever be the same again.
